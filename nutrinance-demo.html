Our Story

Nutrinance was born from a simple frustration.

Every diet plan I came across asked people to give up the food they grew up with. Quinoa instead of khichdi. Salads instead of sabzi. It worked for two weeks — and then real life happened.

So I built something different. Nutrinance is nutrition that lives inside your own kitchen: your family’s recipes, your festivals, your budget, your work hours. We adjust the food you already eat instead of replacing it — which is exactly why our clients actually stay consistent.

Today we work with women managing PCOS, new mothers rebuilding strength, kids who refuse vegetables, and grandparents who want to stay independent. Different goals, same belief: healthy should feel like home.

FAQ

Questions we get every day

Will I have to give up rice, roti and my regular food?

Never. Nutrinance plans are built around desi food. We adjust portions, timing and combinations instead of banning entire food groups — that is exactly why our clients stick with it.

Is the plan customised or a ready-made chart?

Fully customised. Every plan is written after reviewing your medical history, reports, activity level, work hours, food preferences and even your family cooking style.

Do you take clients outside my city?

Yes. Everything runs online over WhatsApp and video calls, so we work with clients across India and abroad.

I have PCOS and thyroid together. Can you still help?

Absolutely — overlapping hormonal conditions are one of our main specialities. Your plan will account for both, along with any medication you are taking.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice better energy and less bloating within two weeks. Visible weight or inch change usually starts from week three or four, depending on your starting point.

What does a program cost?

It depends on the program and duration you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share the current packages after understanding your goal.
